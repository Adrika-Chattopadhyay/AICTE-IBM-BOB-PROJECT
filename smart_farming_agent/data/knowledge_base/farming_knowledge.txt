SECTION 1: KHARIF CROPS (June-October)
========================================
Kharif crops are sown at the beginning of the monsoon season (June-July) and harvested in autumn (September-October).

Major Kharif Crops in India:
- Rice (Paddy): Requires 1000-2000mm rainfall. Major varieties: IR-64, Swarna, Jaya, HMT. Yield: 3-6 tons/ha.
- Cotton: Requires 600-900mm rainfall. Bt cotton recommended for bollworm resistance. Yield: 2-4 bales/ha.
- Soybean: Requires 600-900mm rainfall. Varieties: JS-335, MACS-58. Protein-rich, good export demand. Yield: 2-3 tons/ha.
- Maize: Requires 600-900mm rainfall. Hybrid seeds give 30% higher yield. Yield: 4-8 tons/ha.
- Groundnut: Requires 500-750mm rainfall. Oil content 45-55%. Varieties: GG-2, JL-24.
- Sugarcane: Requires 1500-2000mm rainfall. Ratoon crop possible for 2-3 years.
- Jowar (Sorghum): Drought tolerant. Requires 400-650mm rainfall. Dual purpose (grain + fodder).
- Bajra (Pearl Millet): Very drought tolerant. Grows in sandy soils. Requires 250-400mm rainfall.
- Arhar (Pigeon Pea): Nitrogen-fixing legume. Requires 600-1000mm rainfall.
- Moong: Short duration (60-75 days). Nitrogen-fixing. Requires 600-800mm rainfall.

SECTION 2: RABI CROPS (October-March)
=======================================
Rabi crops are sown after the monsoon (October-November) and harvested in spring (March-April).

Major Rabi Crops:
- Wheat: Ideal temperature 15-20°C. Varieties: HD-2967, GW-322, PBW-343. Yield: 4-6 tons/ha.
- Mustard (Rapeseed): Oil content 40-44%. Varieties: Varuna, Pusa Bold. Yield: 1.5-2.5 tons/ha.
- Gram (Chickpea): Protein-rich (20-22%). Varieties: JG-11, Vijay. Yield: 1.5-2.5 tons/ha.
- Peas: Short duration. Cool weather crop. Requires 60-90 frost-free days.
- Barley: Drought and frost tolerant. Beer and malt industry demand.
- Sunflower: Oil content 40-50%. Can be grown in both kharif and rabi.
- Linseed (Flaxseed): Cold tolerant. Oil used in paints and varnishes.

SECTION 3: SOIL MANAGEMENT
============================
Soil pH Ranges:
- 4.5-5.5: Strongly acidic (add lime to correct)
- 5.5-6.5: Moderately acidic (ideal for tea, coffee)
- 6.5-7.5: Near neutral (ideal for most crops)
- 7.5-8.5: Alkaline (add gypsum/sulfur to correct)
- 8.5+: Strongly alkaline (difficult to farm)

Nutrient Management:
- Nitrogen (N): Applied as Urea (46% N) or DAP (18% N). Promotes vegetative growth.
- Phosphorus (P): Applied as DAP (46% P) or SSP (16% P). Promotes root development.
- Potassium (K): Applied as MOP (60% K) or SOP (50% K). Improves disease resistance.
- Zinc: Deficiency common in India. Apply ZnSO4 @ 25 kg/ha.
- Boron: Deficiency in oilseeds. Apply borax @ 2 kg/ha.
- Sulfur: Deficiency in mustard-growing areas. Apply gypsum @ 200 kg/ha.

SECTION 4: IRRIGATION MANAGEMENT
===================================
Water Requirement by Crop (mm per season):
- Rice: 900-2500mm
- Wheat: 400-500mm
- Cotton: 600-900mm
- Sugarcane: 1500-2000mm
- Groundnut: 400-600mm
- Soybean: 450-700mm

Irrigation Methods:
1. Flood/Surface irrigation: Cheapest, least efficient (40-60% efficiency)
2. Sprinkler irrigation: Good for wheat, groundnut (65-75% efficiency)
3. Drip irrigation: Best for horticulture, sugarcane (85-95% efficiency)
4. Micro-sprinkler: Good for orchards and nurseries

Critical Growth Stages Requiring Irrigation:
- Rice: Tillering, panicle initiation, grain filling
- Wheat: Crown root initiation (21 DAS), jointing, heading
- Cotton: Flowering and boll development

SECTION 5: PEST AND DISEASE MANAGEMENT
========================================
Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Principles:
1. Use resistant varieties
2. Cultural practices (crop rotation, trap crops)
3. Biological control (Trichogramma, Bacillus thuringiensis)
4. Chemical control as last resort

Common Pests:
- Stem Borer (Rice): Use Chlorpyrifos or Cartap Hydrochloride. Release Trichogramma.
- Bollworm (Cotton): Use Bt sprays or chemical Spinosad.
- Whitefly (Cotton, Tomato): Use Imidacloprid or yellow sticky traps.
- Aphids: Use Dimethoate or neem oil.
- Thrips: Use Spinosad or fipronil.
- Leaf Folder (Rice): Use Monocrotophos or Chlorpyrifos.

Common Diseases:
- Rice Blast: Use Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 6g/kg seed treatment.
- Wheat Rust: Use Propiconazole 25 EC @ 0.1%.
- Late Blight (Potato/Tomato): Use Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L.
- Powdery Mildew: Use Wettable Sulphur 80 WP @ 3 g/L.
- Downy Mildew: Use Metalaxyl-Mancozeb.

SECTION 6: GOVERNMENT SCHEMES 2024
=====================================
1. PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi:
   - Benefit: Rs 6000/year (3 installments of Rs 2000)
   - Eligibility: Small/marginal farmers (<2 ha)
   - Website: pmkisan.gov.in

2. PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY):
   - Premium: 1.5% for Rabi, 2% for Kharif, 5% for Horticulture
   - Coverage: Loss due to natural calamities, pests, diseases
   - Registration: Through bank/CSC before cut-off date

3. Kisan Credit Card (KCC):
   - Credit: Up to Rs 3 lakh at 4% interest
   - Valid: 5 years with annual renewal
   - Apply: Nearest bank branch

4. PM KUSUM Scheme:
   - Subsidy: 60-90% on solar pumps
   - Target: 35 lakh solar agricultural pumps
   - Apply: State nodal agency

5. Soil Health Card:
   - Free soil testing every 2 years
   - 12 parameters including NPK, pH, micronutrients
   - Apply: Nearest KVK or Agriculture Department

6. National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA):
   - Promotes water-use efficiency
   - Supports micro-irrigation
   - Soil health management practices
